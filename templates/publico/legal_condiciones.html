    01. Purpose and legal nature of Jobdevs

    Jobdevs is a digital infrastructure technology platform designed to connect legal entities and individuals with software development needs (hereinafter, "Companies") with apprentices and graduates of the Servicio Nacional de Aprendizaje — SENA (hereinafter, "Developers"), enabling project publishing, formalization of agreements through digital contracts, milestone tracking with verifiable evidence, and the building of provable reputation.

    Jobdevs acts exclusively as a provider of technological tools and a traceability environment. Accordingly, Jobdevs is not an employment agency, does not perform labor intermediation, is not an employer or contracting party of the Developers, and does not guarantee any employment relationship, apprenticeship contract, or subordination between Jobdevs and platform users.

    Essential clarification regarding payments and non-retention of funds: Jobdevs is NOT a payment gateway, does NOT receive, hold, custody, or administer money (there is no escrow or security deposit), does NOT make payments or disbursements to the apprentice or Developer, and does not intermediate financial transactions between the parties. Any contractual, financial, or service-provision relationship is entered into and settled directly, autonomously, and independently between the Company and the Developer outside the Platform.

    The scope of Jobdevs is strictly limited to validating technical and documentary traceability (identity, SENA affiliation, Digital Contract, milestones, and evidence) and to verifying and intervening operationally only when conflicts or disputes arise between the parties.

    02. Operational definitions

    For the purposes of interpreting and applying these Terms of Service, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

    • Platform: The website, dashboards, messaging modules, electronic signature tools, milestone management features, and APIs operated under the Jobdevs brand.
    • Company: A legally incorporated legal entity or registered individual merchant duly identified with a NIT or Colombian National ID (Cédula de Ciudadanía) that publishes software requirements on the Platform.
    • Developer: An active apprentice or graduate of SENA technology or software development training programs who registers to apply for and execute projects.
    • Project: A software development requirement published by a Company specifying technical scope, available openings, suggested technologies, and estimated timelines.
    • Digital Contract: An electronic document generated on the Platform setting forth the terms agreed between the Company and the Developer, executed through a double electronic signature with an integrity seal.
    • Milestone and Evidence: A verifiable unit of progress within a Project, accompanied by technical deliverables, repository links, screenshots, or files uploaded to the Platform's private storage.
    • Dispute: A formal controversy procedure initiated by either party to a Digital Contract in the event of breach, technical disagreement, or non-consensual cancellation request, subject to administrative mediation.

    03. Eligibility, registration, and identity verification

    Access to Jobdevs operational features requires creating a personal or corporate account and providing truthful, complete, and verifiable information. By registering, the user represents and warrants that they have legal capacity to enter into binding obligations and that all documentation submitted is authentic.

    3.1. Validation of identity and tax documents

    • Developers: May register exclusively with a valid Colombian National ID (Cédula de Ciudadanía — CC) or Identity Card (Tarjeta de Identidad — TI). The platform validates document format, uniqueness, and the minimum age requirements established for registration.
    • Companies: May register with a NIT (Tax Identification Number) — subject to mathematical verification of the check digit pursuant to the official DIAN algorithm — or with a Cédula de Ciudadanía when acting as a natural person.
    • SENA academic verification: Developers may verify their affiliation using their official SENA cohort number (ficha) and training program. Verification may be performed automatically against the authorized institutional registry or through documentary review by the administrative team.

    3.2. Operating modes: Pilot Mode vs. Institutional Mode

    Jobdevs may operate under two administrative modalities depending on the deployment environment:

    • Pilot Mode: Allows open public registration of Companies and Developers. In this mode, accounts that remain without completed identity or academic verification and without associated contractual activity within three (3) calendar days from creation may be automatically purged and deleted by the system in order to preserve ecosystem integrity and prevent inactive or fraudulent accounts.
    • Institutional Mode: Account provisioning for apprentices and companies is managed directly by authorized institutional administrators, and the open public registration form may be restricted.

    The use of altered documents, impersonation of SENA cohorts, or submission of false information entitles Jobdevs to reject verification, limit verification attempts, and immediately suspend or terminate the account.

    4.1. Common obligations

    • Maintain the confidentiality of access credentials and immediately report any unauthorized use of the account.
    • Act in good faith, professionally, and respectfully in all internal communications, negotiations, deliveries, and evaluations.
    • Fully comply with applicable Colombian laws on civil, commercial, tax, intellectual property, and personal data protection matters.
    • Refrain from circumventing the Platform's traceability, documentary verification, or evidence-logging mechanisms.

    4.2. Specific obligations of Companies

    • Draft clear, lawful, and achievable specifications when publishing projects, without demanding disguised unpaid work or abusive technical tests outside the agreed scope.
    • Review deliverables and milestones submitted by Developers in a timely manner and provide objective, technical, and well-founded feedback.
    • Honor the terms agreed in the Digital Contract, including timelines, scope, and compensation arranged directly with the Developer.

    4.3. Specific obligations of Developers

    • Apply only to projects for which they have genuine availability and appropriate technical skills, respecting the active limit on simultaneous applications on the Platform.
    • Deliver original, documented, and verifiable code for each milestone, refraining from plagiarizing third-party protected software or violating open-source licenses.
    • Promptly communicate any technical impediment or change in availability to the Company through the project's official channels.

    05. Project publishing, applications, and negotiation

    Verified Companies may publish projects detailing functional descriptions, suggested technologies, number of openings, and estimated schedules. Jobdevs reserves the right to moderate, pause, or remove listings that are unlawful, misleading, discriminatory, or incompatible with the educational and professional purpose of the Platform.

    Verified Developers may apply by attaching their technical cover letter and professional profile. Once a Company shortlists or accepts an application, the negotiation and Digital Contract formalization phase is enabled. Negotiations or proposals exceeding the maximum response deadlines configured on the Platform without a reply from either party may automatically expire to release the opening and preserve process agility.

    06. Binding Digital Contracts and double electronic signature

    When a Company and a Developer agree to execute a project, Jobdevs generates a Digital Contract consolidating the identification of the parties, technical scope, schedule, agreed milestones, and essential execution clauses.

    Execution of the Digital Contract takes place through a double electronic signature (authenticated acceptance by both the Company and the Developer). Pursuant to Law 527 of 1999 (which defines and regulates access to and use of data messages and e-commerce in Colombia) and Decree 2364 of 2012, data messages and electronic signatures generated within Jobdevs hold full legal validity and evidentiary force between the parties.

    • Integrity and traceability: Each signature records the authenticated identity of the signer, exact timestamp, and a cryptographic integrity seal over the contractual document.
    • Professional evidence snapshot: Upon contract execution, the Platform preserves a snapshot of the parties' profiles and current verification status to guarantee legal certainty throughout execution.
    • Immutability: Signed contracts and their audit logs cannot be unilaterally altered. Any substantial amendment requires formal documentation through the mechanisms provided on the Platform.

    07. Milestone execution and verifiable evidence

    Project execution on Jobdevs is structured around verifiable milestones. For each milestone, the Developer must record the corresponding progress along with a technical description, links to repositories or deployments, and evidence files backed up in the Platform's private storage.

    Evidence files are subject to technical validation of file extension, size, and structure. The Developer warrants that uploaded evidence reflects actual work performed and is free of malicious code or unauthorized third-party personal data. Overall project progress percentage is calculated exclusively from milestones formally approved by the Company.

    08. Review, approval, disputes, and cancellations

    8.1. Deliverable review and approval

    Once progress is uploaded, the Company may use the Platform's channels to approve the milestone or request specific, objective revisions within the scope agreed in the Digital Contract. Observations must be stated clearly, verifiably, and respectfully.

    8.2. Cancellation requests

    Either party may request early cancellation of a contract by stating the cause and supporting justification. Where mutual agreement exists, closure is formalized with a record of the progress achieved. Absent consensus, the case may be escalated to administrative review or formal dispute.

    8.3. Dispute system and mediation

    In the event of serious controversies involving non-delivery, project abandonment, unjustified rejection of milestones, or breach of the Digital Contract, either party may open a formal Dispute within the Platform:

    • Upon opening a dispute, a private mediation room is enabled between the Company, the Developer, and an authorized Jobdevs administrator.
    • The parties may submit statements and documentary evidence (PDF, ZIP, PNG, JPG/JPEG within established limits) supporting their position.
    • The administrator will review contract traceability, recorded milestones, uploaded evidence, and relevant communications to issue an administrative resolution within the Jobdevs ecosystem (closure, cancellation, continuation, or account measures).
    • Jobdevs mediation is an operational documentary-validation mechanism to resolve conflicts within the Platform: it does not involve holding, releasing, or transferring money and does not replace ordinary courts or alternative dispute resolution mechanisms provided by Colombian law.

    09. Mutual rating and verifiable public reputation

    Upon successful completion of a project and closure of the Digital Contract, both the Company and the Developer may submit a mutual rating (numeric star scale and qualitative comment). These ratings build the verifiable public reputation displayed on Platform profiles.

    Ratings must be based solely on actual performance during the project. Submitting false, defamatory, discriminatory, coerced reviews, or engaging in fraudulent reputation exchange is strictly prohibited. Jobdevs may moderate or remove comments containing offensive language, disclosing sensitive personal data, or violating these Terms, recording such action in the audit log.

    Jobdevs actúa exclusivamente como proveedor de herramientas tecnológicas y entorno de trazabilidad. En consecuencia, Jobdevs no es una agencia de empleo, no realiza intermediación laboral, no es empleador ni contratante de los Desarrolladores, ni garantiza relación laboral, contrato de aprendizaje o subordinación alguna entre Jobdevs y los usuarios de la plataforma.

    Aclaración esencial sobre pagos y no retención de dinero: Jobdevs NO es una pasarela de pagos, NO recibe, retiene, custodia ni administra dinero (no existe depósito en garantía ni escrow), NO realiza pagos ni desembolsos al aprendiz o Desarrollador y no intermedia transacciones económicas entre las partes. Toda relación contractual, económica o de prestación de servicios se celebra y liquida de manera directa, autónoma e independiente entre la Empresa y el Desarrollador fuera de la Plataforma.

    El alcance de Jobdevs se limita exclusivamente a validar la trazabilidad técnica y documental (identidad, vinculación SENA, Contrato Digital, hitos y evidencias) y a verificar e intervenir operativamente únicamente cuando se presenten conflictos o disputas entre las partes.

    02. Definiciones operativas

    • Plataforma: El sitio web, paneles de control, módulos de mensajería, firma digital, gestión de hitos y APIs operadas bajo la marca Jobdevs.
    • Empresa: Persona jurídica constituida legalmente o persona natural comerciante debidamente identificada con NIT o Cédula de Ciudadanía que publica requerimientos de software en la Plataforma.
    • Desarrollador: Aprendiz activo o egresado de programas de formación del SENA del área de tecnología o desarrollo de software que se registra para postularse y ejecutar proyectos.

    03. Elegibilidad, registro y verificación de identidad

    El acceso a las funcionalidades operativas de Jobdevs requiere la creación de una cuenta personal o corporativa y el suministro de información veraz, completa y verificable. Al registrarse, el usuario declara y garantiza que cuenta con capacidad legal para obligarse y que toda la documentación suministrada es auténtica.

    3.1. Validación de documentos de identidad y tributarios

    • Desarrolladores: Pueden registrarse únicamente con Cédula de Ciudadanía (CC) o Tarjeta de Identidad (TI) válida en Colombia. La plataforma valida el formato, la unicidad del documento y los requisitos mínimos de edad establecidos para el registro.
    • Empresas: Pueden registrarse con NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) —sujeto a verificación matemática del dígito de verificación conforme al algoritmo oficial de la DIAN— o con Cédula de Ciudadanía cuando actúen como persona natural.
    • Verificación académica SENA: Los Desarrolladores pueden acreditar su vinculación mediante su número de ficha oficial y programa de formación SENA.     3.2. Modos de operación: Modo Piloto vs. Modo Institucional

    Jobdevs puede operar bajo dos modalidades administrativas según el entorno de despliegue:

    • Modo Piloto: Permite el registro público abierto de Empresas y Desarrolladores. En este modo, las cuentas que permanezcan sin completar su verificación de identidad o académica y sin actividad contractual asociada dentro de un plazo de tres (3) días calendario contados desde su creación podrán ser depuradas y eliminadas automáticamente por el sistema con el fin de preservar la integridad del ecosistema y prevenir cuentas inactivas o fraudulentas.
    • Modo Institucional: El alta de cuentas de aprendices y empresas es gestionada directamente por los administradores institucionales autorizados, pudiendo restringirse el formulario de registro público abierto.

    El uso de documentos adulterados, suplantación de fichas SENA o suministro de información falsa faculta a Jobdevs a rechazar la verificación, limitar intentos de solicitud y suspender o cancelar la cuenta de forma inmediata.

Las negociaciones o propuestas que excedan los plazos máximos configurados en la Plataforma sin respuesta de alguna de las partes podrán expirar automáticamente para liberar la vacante y mantener la agilidad del proceso.

    06. Contratos Digitales vinculantes y doble firma electrónica

    Cuando una Empresa y un Desarrollador acuerdan ejecutar un proyecto, Jobdevs genera un Contrato Digital que consolida la identificación de las partes, el alcance técnico, el cronograma, los hitos acordados y las cláusulas esenciales de ejecución.

    El perfeccionamiento del Contrato Digital se realiza mediante doble firma electrónica (aceptación autenticada de la Empresa y del Desarrollador). De conformidad con la Ley 527 de 1999 (por medio de la cual se define y reglamenta el acceso y uso de los mensajes de datos y el comercio electrónico en Colombia) y el Decreto 2364 de 2012, los mensajes de datos y las firmas electrónicas generadas dentro de Jobdevs tienen plena validez jurídica y fuerza probatoria entre las partes.

    • Integridad y trazabilidad: Cada firma registra la identidad autenticada del firmante, marca de tiempo exacta y sello criptográfico de integridad sobre el documento contractual.
    • Congelamiento de evidencia profesional: Al perfeccionarse el contrato, la Plataforma preserva una instantánea del perfil y estado de verificación vigente de las partes para garantizar seguridad jurídica durante toda la ejecución.
    • Inalterabilidad: Los contratos firmados y sus registros de auditoría no pueden ser alterados unilateralmente. Cualquier modificación sustancial requiere constancia formal conforme a los mecanismos previstos en la Plataforma.

    07. Ejecución por hitos y evidencias verificables

    La ejecución de los proyectos en Jobdevs se estructura por hitos verificables. Por cada hito, el Desarrollador debe registrar el avance correspondiente adjuntando descripción técnica, enlaces a repositorios o despliegues y archivos de evidencia respaldados en el almacenamiento privado de la Plataforma.

    Los archivos de evidencia están sujetos a validaciones técnicas de extensión, tamaño y estructura. El Desarrollador garantiza que las evidencias cargadas corresponden al trabajo real ejecutado y que están libres de código malicioso o datos personales no autorizados de terceros. El avance porcentual del proyecto se calcula exclusivamente a partir de los hitos formalmente aprobados por la Empresa.

    08. Revisión, aprobación, disputas y cancelaciones

    8.1. Revisión y aprobación de entregas

    Una vez cargado un avance, la Empresa dispone de los canales de la Plataforma para aprobar el hito o solicitar correcciones puntuales y objetivas dentro del alcance pactado en el Contrato Digital. Las observaciones deben formularse de manera clara, verificable y respetuosa.

    8.2. Solicitudes de cancelación

    Cualquiera de las partes puede solicitar la cancelación anticipada de un contrato exponiendo la causal y justificación correspondiente. Cuando exista mutuo acuerdo, el cierre se formaliza dejando constancia del estado de avance alcanzado. A falta de consenso, el caso puede escalarse a revisión administrativa o disputa formal.

    8.3. Sistema de disputas y mediación

    Ante controversias graves relacionadas con incumplimiento de entregables, abandono del proyecto, rechazo injustificado de hitos o vulneración del Contrato Digital, cualquiera de las partes podrá abrir una Disputa formal dentro de la Plataforma:
